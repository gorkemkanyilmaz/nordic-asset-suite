# Nordic Asset Suite — App Store Optimization (ASO) & Keyword Strategy

## 1. Growth Model (Zero Ads, 100% High-Intent Organic Search)
The Nordic Asset Suite strictly forbids paid ad acquisition or in-app ads. Growth is powered entirely by high-intent, long-tail App Store search queries centered around the formula:

$$\text{Search Intent} = \text{Brand} + \text{Model} + \text{Maintenance / Warranty / Problem}$$
